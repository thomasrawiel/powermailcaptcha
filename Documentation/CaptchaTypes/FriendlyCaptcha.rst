============
Friendly Captcha V1 + V2
============

- Register your domain to https://docs.friendlycaptcha.com/#/installation
- Add sitekey and secretkey to the :ref:`Typoscript constants <typoscript>`
- In TypoScript Constants, set the captcha method to `friendlycaptcha` or `friendlycaptcha2` for V1 or V2 respectively

- If your account allows enabling the EU endpoint, you can change the `Verify Url Endpoint` typoscript constant to `eu`
